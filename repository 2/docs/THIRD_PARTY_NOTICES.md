# Third-party and ownership review

UNI2-h is an external dependency; obtain its code/weights and applicable terms from its original provider. No UNI2-h weights are redistributed. Other dependencies retain their own licenses. Original notices present in copied source are retained; absence of a notice is not proof of ownership or permission. This staging package intentionally grants no new license. Confirm authorship, institutional rights and third-party publication eligibility before public release.
